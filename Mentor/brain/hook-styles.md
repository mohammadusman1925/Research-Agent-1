## Hook Styles, the locked 9

**Negation Chain**
> "The most dangerous sign of [CONDITION] is not [X]. It is not [Y]. It is not even [Z]."

Build tension across three denials before revealing the real answer.

**Credential Lead**
> "I'm a [TITLE] with [YEARS] years. And if your [METRIC] is between [RANGE], your [EXPERT] is watching one number and missing another."

Authority first, then the thing the authority reveals.

**Failed Quest**
> "I [ACTION] every day for [TIME]. Never missed a dose. Tracked my [METRIC]. My numbers stayed [BAD]."

Exhaustive effort followed by zero result, earns permission to explain why.

**Historical**
> "[TIME PERIOD] ago, [HISTORICAL FIGURES] were [ACHIEVING RESULT] that modern [FIELD] says is [IMPOSSIBLE]."

Recovered knowledge framing. Mythic and calm. Lowers sales resistance before the mechanism arrives.

**Confrontation**
> "I'm about to say something that might annoy [TARGET GROUP]."

Direct challenge signalling the reader is about to hear something forbidden.

**Symptom Reframe**
> "[VISIBLE SYMPTOM] is not a [ASSUMED CATEGORY] problem. It is a [REAL CATEGORY] warning."

Takes something they can see and relocates its cause.

**Caregiver Hook**
> "My [RELATION] had been taking [PRODUCT] for exactly [SPECIFIC DAYS] days when [EVENT]."

The precision of the day count creates instant credibility.

**Impossible Result**
> "My [SPECIALIST] called and said the lab messed up. They ran it twice."

Third-party disbelief as proof.

**Empathy + Confrontation**

Empathy for their situation first, then confront with the uncomfortable truth.

## Awareness Stages, the locked 5

| Stage | Lead with |
|---|---|
| Unaware | The problem or the story. Product comes late. |
| Problem Aware | Name the problem sharply, then imply the solution. |
| Solution Aware | The desire, or the mechanism that makes us different. |
| Product Aware | Strongest benefit, new proof, new reason to believe. |
| Most Aware | The product and the offer itself. |

Always paired with **sophistication**, how many similar claims this market has already heard. A saturated market needs a new mechanism or a fresh angle to be believed at all. See `CORE-1-PRINCIPLES.md`, Pillar 4.

## The persona pattern

The brand's actual narrators go in `01-BRAND-SETUP.md`. This is the pattern they are built on, which is the part that transfers.

### FIXED / FREE

The rule that makes a narrator reusable across dozens of ads without becoming a continuity trap:

```
FIXED -- identical in every ad, never varied
  Name, credential, years
  Career path
  Voice and register
  Sign-off
  [PLATFORM] the page they run on

FREE -- belongs to the ad, not to the persona
  Their numbers. Their family. The discovery.
  The low point. The timeline.
```

**Why it is built this way.** A heavily-used narrator will accumulate contradictions across shipped work, different numbers, different ages, different families. Reconciling them means rewriting things that already ran. Fixing the *profile* and freeing the *story* removes the conflict instead of resolving it.

### Every persona carries three things

| | |
|---|---|
| **Signature move** | The structural thing only this narrator does. *Names a pattern seen hundreds of times, then reveals she is in it. Lists what he wrongly concluded about himself before explaining what was actually wrong. Names the loss in occasions, never in symptoms.* |
| **Stated limits** | What they can never say or be. Write these down, they are what prevents misuse. *No profanity and no conspiracy. Never blames a person. Never recommends a product to a patient. Is on no medication and starts none.* |
| **Cannot carry** | Sources this persona structurally cannot narrate. A narrator fixed at 38 years' experience cannot front a story built on an 11-year career. |

### Four rules learned expensively

1. **Never create a second narrator with the same role.** One nurse, one pharmacist, one specialist. A second one always ends up blended with the first.
2. **A career path is what makes a persona flexible.** "ICU, then med-surg, then cardiac step-down" credibly covers three completely different sources without inventing anybody new.
3. **The refusal of a credential can *be* the credential.** The narrator who opens by telling you she is nobody is often the strongest one available in a market tired of experts.
4. **Numbers in a shipped ad are permanent.** A new ad that contradicts them contradicts something already running. That may be worth spending, but spend it knowingly.
