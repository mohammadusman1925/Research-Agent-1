Beat 10 is the only honest test. The iteration and the use case swap leave the mechanism completely alone. The angle swap rewrites it. If you set out to change the angle and beat 10 still reads the same, you did not change the angle.

Sixteen of seventeen beats are copy and paste in an iteration.

Nine of seventeen even in the biggest move. Most of a working ad never changes, and that is the point.
