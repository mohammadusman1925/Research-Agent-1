**New**

Not about the pain. I was used to the pain.

I was sitting there counting. Two left. Maybe a year of feeling normal, spread out in pieces.

And a date at the end of it that I never chose.

## The image one per script

Every image in this account so far is text on a plain background. Zero symptom images have ever run, despite the copy handing over the sock, the letter, the heat pack. So each direction below is untested here. One image per script, no backups, no options. The method's own rule: show the most interesting concrete thing in the script. Never an idea, never a text overlay, and on cold traffic never the product, because the belt has not been earned yet.

The rule. The image comes from whatever the hook names. Not from the use case as a category, not from the pain point as a category. From the hook. That is why the method says finish the headline first: a changed headline voids its image direction.

| If the hook names | The image comes from | Beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The situation she is in | The use case | 1 and 6 |
| A thing she can no longer do | The pain point | 4 |
| Why the last treatment failed | The explanation | 10 |
| A number or a result | The proof | 14 |
| The offer or the price | The product | 13 |

The gate that overrules it. Awareness. If the two disagree, awareness wins. Problem aware and cold: the use case or the pain point, and never the product because it has not been earned. Solution aware: the explanation, the diagram, the depth. Most aware: the product and the offer, never a symptom. Every script on this page is problem aware, so the belt never appears.

The pattern, four steps. 1. Read the hook. 2. Circle every noun you could photograph. 3. If there is more than one, take the most specific and the least expected. 4. That noun is the image, alone in the frame, nothing else.

## KTA act on these

- Write the use case as a name plus a scene, in one line. If you cannot picture the room, it is a segment, not a use case.
- Choose the new scene by the feeling, not the topic. Same emotion, different situation. That is what keeps the test clean.
- Freeze the belief shift word for word. Beats 8, 10, 12 and 13 are copy and paste. They are the control.
- Expect five to six beats to move, not two. The scene runs through the hook, the failed attempts, the low point, the searches, the proof and the close.
- Do not fix other gaps while you are here. One variable.
- Name it NN, not ITE. A new scene is a new concept.
- Judge on CPA. Not reactions, not comments.
