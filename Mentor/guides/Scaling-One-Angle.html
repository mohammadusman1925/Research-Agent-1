The rule
Stays the same in all fiveMaggie. Denise. The mechanism. The product. The guarantee. The results. Word for word.
ChangesThe hook. One line in the pattern beat. The 2am search. The CTA. The close. The image.
How many scenesThree to five under one angle.
When to spendWhen two of them beat baseline. One is luck.
When to find a new angleWhen four or five have failed.

Every scene below is already in the script you wrote. None of them are new ideas. They are buried at beats 3, 4, 6 and 7 where only the people who read that far ever see them.
