# Angle Swap

**Same woman. Same scene. A different reason it never healed.**

Control Wrong depth, starved tissue
 Test The question nobody asked
 Moves 2 layers, 8 beats

## The swap 9 layers

| Layer | Control | Test |   | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Audience, budget, page, LP |   | Identical | KEEP | Same everything downstream |

Two rows move, and they are the same row really. The angle is the belief shift and the emotion together. Compare the shape with the other two guides: the use case swap moved the scene and left the belief alone. This does the exact opposite.

Eight beats, and that is the tell. An angle swap is the biggest of the three tests. Use case swap moved six. An iteration moved one. If you counted eight here and only two in your own draft, the angle did not really change.

## The image one per script

The rule. The image comes from whatever the hook names. If the hook has no photographable noun, go up one step to the scene it came from and take an object out of that. Never invent an object that is not in the copy.

| Test the question nobody asked | "Four appointments in eighteen months. Every one of them looked at the scan." | Four appointment cards fanned out on the kitchen bench, dates spread across eighteen months, every one for the same hip. Nothing else in frame. No person, no product. | 2 | The hook has no photographable noun in it, so the rule says go up one step to the scene and take an object out of it. Four cards is the argument in one glance: she did everything she was told, four times, and it went nowhere. If it beats the letter, being unassessed is a stronger entry than being on a list. |

## KTA act on these

- The angle is the belief shift and the emotion together. Move one and the other moves with it. That is normal and it is still one variable.
- The scene does not move. Same woman, same 14 month wait, same driveway, same date. If the scene moved too, you wrote a new concept, not an angle test.
- Beat 10 is the swap. Everything else that changed only changed to stay consistent with it.
