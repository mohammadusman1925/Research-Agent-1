The image one per script

The rule. The image comes from whatever the hook names. If the hook has no photographable noun, go up one step to the scene it came from and take an object out of that. Never invent an object that is not in the copy.

KTA act on these

  1. The angle is the belief shift and the emotion together. Move one and the other moves with it. That is normal and it is still one variable.
  2. The scene does not move. Same woman, same 14 month wait, same driveway, same date. If the scene moved too, you wrote a new concept, not an angle test.
  3. Beat 10 is the swap. Everything else that changed only changed to stay consistent with it.
  4. Hold awareness at problem aware on purpose. Reframe angles drift solution aware, and if awareness drifts, the landing page stops matching the ad.
  5. Keep the identity validation and drop the antagonism. The line is that the assessment was incomplete, never that male clinicians cannot understand women. That guardrail is in the account's own core memory.
  6. Name it NN. A new belief shift is a new concept.
  7. Judge on CPA against the control, and watch the comments. A reframe angle either lands hard or gets argued with, and both are information.

Keep the moment. Change the reason.

The scene stays exactly where it was. What moves is why she was never fixed, and everything downstream shifts to stay consistent with it.
