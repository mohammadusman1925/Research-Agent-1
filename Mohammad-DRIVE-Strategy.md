## Part 1: Discover

Your prospect's real problem isn't bad ads. It's that their creative person and their media person don't talk to each other — and they're paying for both.

### The Real Insight

Your LinkedIn "About" nails something nobody else in the market says: **the handoff problem.** Freelancers sell creative OR media. Agencies sell both but silo them internally. You're the only person saying: "These two things are one system. I run both. Learning compounds instead of leaking." That's the positioning weapon.

### What Your Prospect's Week Actually Looks Like

> "I constantly had to tell them what to test or change. Their designers were good at following instructions, but there was very little actual strategy or creative direction coming from them." — r/ecommerce, June 2026

> "I genuinely cannot tell whether the issue is: creative angles, offer positioning, ASC/broad structure, Meta traffic quality, account quality/data, landing page mismatch, or just creative fatigue." — r/ecommerce, June 2026

> "I've paid two consultants. Both took a retainer and did basically nothing. So I'm done with flat retainers." — r/FacebookAds, July 2026

The founder's problem isn't the retainer. It's that they paid for execution, not for someone who thinks. They had two people doing half a job each. The ad account never compounded.

### The Four Things They Already Know (And One They Don't)

1. They know their business. They built it. Don't explain their market back to them.
2. They run their own creatives. They've been in Canva at midnight. They know what a hook is.
3. They want plug-and-play. They don't want to manage you. They want to hand you the keys and see results.
4. They want someone who does real research. Not vibes. Not competitor screenshots. Actual competitive intelligence.

**The one thing they DON'T know:** The reason nothing compounds is that creative and media are running in parallel, not as one system. Nobody told them the handoff is where the money leaks.

### The Mass Desire

"I want ONE person who runs both creative and ads — so I stop paying two people to do half a job each, and my ad account actually compounds."

### The Unified System (Your Mechanism)

**1. Creative Strategy & Briefing** — Pull signal from Meta Ad Library, MagicBriefs, TryAtria, Kalodata, AdSpy. Briefs ship against a thesis, not a guess. AI research pipeline produces 50+ angles, competitive analysis, customer voice mining — before a dollar goes into production.

**2. Meta Ads at Scale** — Disciplined budget pacing, creative rotation, audience strategy across multiple Shopify accounts. TripleWhale for attribution so decisions protect profit, not surface ROAS.

**3. Full-Funnel Diagnostics** — Ads are only one leak. Landing pages, offer mechanics, lifecycle email — audit where revenue actually falls out of the funnel.

**4. VSL & UGC Scripting** — Hook discipline, story structure, CTA design. Every script reviewed against the copywriting system before it ships.

**5. AI-Powered Production** — Higgsfield, Hedra, Adobe Firefly, Freepik, Claude, ChatGPT, Manus workflows. Concept-to-launch compressed without sacrificing quality.

**6. Creative Ops & Team** — Hire, train, lead editors, designers, VAs on SOPs built in ClickUp and Notion. Output stays consistent as the team scales.

**"$3,500 is a lot. How do I know it's worth it?"**
You're currently paying for creative + media separately. A media buyer at $2K/month + a content person at $1.5K/month = $3,500 anyway. Except they don't talk to each other. Same budget, but everything compounds instead of leaking.

**"I've been burned before. Agencies promised strategy and delivered nothing."**

**"Can one person really run both creative and media?"**
I'm not one person. I lead strategists, media buyers, editors, and VAs under one playbook. The difference is: one operator owns the whole system. No handoff. No silo.

**"What about compliance? Health/beauty is a minefield."**
8 health/wellness DTC brands. Zero account bans. Compliance isn't an afterthought — it's built into the research and scripting process.

**"What happens when I stop paying?"**
You keep the system. SOPs in ClickUp/Notion. KPI dashboards. AI workflows. Testing frameworks. The system survives after the engagement.
