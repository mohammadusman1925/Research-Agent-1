*3. SCRIPT DISSECTION*

Every line placed for a reason:

*"I used to hand my kid the iPad just to get 20 minutes of peace"*
- "Used to" = past tense = something changed = curiosity gap
- Normalises shame through confession (Schwartz identification technique)
- 14 words. Two lines on mobile.

*"Every single time, I'd feel that little stab of guilt"*
- Names the emotion they feel but rarely say out loud
- "Little stab" = specific + visceral, not generic "I felt bad"

*"Nothing else held his attention for more than five minutes"*
- Pre-empts viewer's objection before they think it
- Creates open loop: "but clearly something DID..."

*"They'd sit in a cupboard after day one"*
- Surfaces objection #2 BEFORE product intro
- Every parent has a cupboard proving this point

*"Nothing can compete with a screen... or so I thought"*
- THE PIVOT. Problem → solution
