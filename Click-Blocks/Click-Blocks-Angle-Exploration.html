How to read this

  • Guilty Scroller × Screen guilt and Developmental Worrier × Attention/milestones are the two hottest cells — that's where Clusters 1 and 4–5 should get the majority of test budget.
  • Toy Graveyard × Dust/Price/"every brand claims this" is a triple-high row — this ICP needs the most proof-dense creative (Cluster 2 and Cluster 8).
  • ADHD/Neurodiverse × Engagement/Attention is high but narrow — small audience, very high relevance. Don't dilute it with broad screen-guilt messaging (Cluster 4).
  • Gift-Giver only lights up on dust/price — keep gift angles proof-and-value led, not guilt-led (Cluster 6).
