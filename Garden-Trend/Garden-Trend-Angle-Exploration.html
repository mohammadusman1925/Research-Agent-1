Compliance Notes

Risk Levels & Guidelines

  • LOW risk: Comfort claims, value claims, personal experience, lifestyle framing. Standard ad creative — no special disclaimers needed beyond standard advertising standards.
  • MEDIUM risk: Sleep quality improvement claims, "97% first night" stat (brand claim requiring substantiation), temperature/cooling claims (verify specifics), pain reduction framing. Add disclaimers. Frame as personal experience. Avoid absolute language ("cures," "eliminates").
  • HIGH risk: Medical condition treatment claims (fibromyalgia, arthritis, chronic pain), medication reduction claims, doctor endorsement framing. These require: genuine testimonials (not scripted), "results may vary" disclaimers, no treatment/cure language, and ideally verified medical professional endorsements for authority angles.

Platform-Specific Notes

  • Meta Ads: Health claims trigger special ad category review. Frame as comfort/lifestyle, not medical. "Helps you sleep better" is safer than "relieves chronic pain." Personal experience framing ("I noticed...") is safer than universal claims.
  • TikTok: Medical claims will get content suppressed. UGC/testimonial format performs best. "This helped ME" framing. Avoid clinical language. Community/trend framing is the safest approach.
  • YouTube: More room for long-form educational content. Can be more specific about mechanism. Still avoid treatment claims. Disclosure requirements for sponsored/gifted content.
  • FTC Requirements: All testimonials must be genuine. Material connections disclosed. Results-based claims need "results may vary." Any statistics cited must be substantiated and sourced.

Negative Review Mitigation

  • Primary objection: "Thin as a blanket" / doesn't look thick enough on arrival. This is the #1 risk signal from reviews.
  • Creative response: Use unboxing/expansion footage. Show compressed-to-expanded transformation. Address skepticism head-on through "Skeptic to Believer" angles.
  • Guarantee framing: Lead with the return policy / satisfaction guarantee in all ads targeting skeptical personas. If a guarantee exists, make it prominent.
  • Expectation setting: Bamboo fiber toppers work through fiber flexibility, not raw thickness. Educate on mechanism to reframe expectations before purchase.
