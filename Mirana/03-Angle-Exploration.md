**Prepared by:** Mohammad Usman | Creative Strategy System  
**Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Purpose:** Strategic angle mapping for Mirana's creative system — Mira™ Posture Corrector Bra V3  
**Scope:** 7 clusters × 10 angles = 70 total angles with hooks, formats, metrics, and risks

---

## How to Read This Document

Each angle is a strategic direction for creative production. For each, I map:
- **The Angle** — the core creative thesis
- **Why It Works for Mirana** — specific to their product, market position, and customer
- **Creative Formats** — how it plays across video, static, UGC, native
- **Hooks** — opening lines that execute this angle
- **Metrics to Watch** — which KPIs validate or kill this angle
- **Risk** — what could make this angle fail

## Cluster 1: The Comfort Revolution

### 1.1 — "The Last Bra You'll Ever Dread"

**Thesis:** Most women have an adversarial relationship with their bras. Underwires poke. Straps dig. The Mira Bra isn't a posture device — it's the end of bra dread.

**Why It Works:** Reframes the purchase from "health product" to "daily quality of life upgrade." Every woman who rips off her bra the second she gets home is the target audience.

**Formats:**
- **Video/UGC:** "The moment I get home" — peeling off regular bra vs. forgetting Mira is on
- **Static:** Split-screen of marks left by regular bras vs. smooth skin after Mira
- **Carousel:** "Things I don't miss: strap marks, underwire stabbing, adjusting every hour"

**Hooks:**
- "If you hate putting on a bra in the morning, keep watching."
- "The last time I ripped my bra off at the end of the day was [date]."
- "I didn't know bras could feel like this."

**Metrics:** Hook rate, Hold rate, CTR

**Risk:** Must genuinely deliver all-day comfort across cup sizes. Truthfulness is non-negotiable.

**Thesis:** Women want posture support nobody knows about. Mira's seamless design means she gets the benefit without the "medical device" stigma.

**Formats:**

## Cluster 2: The Pain Story

### 2.1 — "The 3pm Backache Is Not Normal"

**Thesis:** Millions of women accept daily back pain as part of being a woman, having a desk job, or having larger breasts. It's normalized but not inevitable. Mira is the intervention that doesn't feel like one.

**Why It Works:** Pain is the #1 purchase driver. Getting specific — "3pm backache," "the commute slouch," "the bedtime shoulder ache" — makes the audience feel seen.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** Day-in-the-life showing exact pain moments and how Mira changes each
- **Static:** Clock graphic — "When does YOUR back give up?"
- **UGC:** Women describing their exact pain moments — raw, unscripted, specific

**Hooks:**
- "If your back starts hurting around 3pm every day, it's not your spine. It's your bra."
- "I spent $3,000 on a chiropractor before I spent $XX on this bra."
- "The pain I thought was permanent turned out to be preventable."
- "My doctor said strengthen your core. My friend said change your bra. My friend was right."

**Metrics:** Hook rate, CPA, Hold rate

**Risk:** Medical claims without clinical backing. Stay in "comfort/support" territory. Compliance-critical.

### 2.2 — "What Your Bra Is Doing to Your Body"

**Thesis:** Educational content about how poorly designed bras contribute to posture problems, shoulder pain, and long-term alignment issues. Position Mira as the root-cause solution.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** Explainer showing how underwire creates pressure points and narrow straps concentrate weight
- **Static:** Infographic — "Your bra vs. your spine"
- **Carousel:** "5 signs your bra is ruining your posture"
- **TikTok:** Quick expert-style breakdown

**Hooks:**
- "Your bra is doing more damage to your posture than your desk chair."
- "Physical therapist here. Let me show you what's happening to your shoulders right now."
- "The shoulder groove from your bra strap isn't just cosmetic — here's what it means."

**Metrics:** Save rate, Share rate, CPM (educational content gets lower CPMs)

**Risk:** Too educational = boring. Lead with a provocative claim, then deliver education.

**Metrics:** AOV (dual purchase), Cross-sell rate (women buying for men and vice versa), Engagement

**Risk:** Can feel gimmicky. Must be executed with genuine couple chemistry, not forced pairing.

### 7.6 — "The Self-Care Sunday Bundle"

**Thesis:** Package Mira + Magic Body Oil as a weekly self-care ritual, not just daily wear. Position it as the indulgent version of body confidence.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** Self-care Sunday montage — bath, body oil, fresh Mira, cozy at-home outfit
- **Static:** "Your Sunday reset. Your Monday confidence."
- **UGC:** Self-care creators integrating Mira and body oil into their routines

**Hooks:**
- "My self-care Sunday now includes posture. Sounds weird. Feels amazing."
- "Oil. Bra. Confidence. The Sunday ritual that fixes Monday."
- "Self-care isn't just face masks and candles. It's how you hold yourself."

**Metrics:** Engagement (self-care content performs well organically), AOV, Brand affinity

**Risk:** Self-care as a marketing concept is oversaturated. Must feel genuine, not like a buzzword bundle.

### 7.7 — "The Starter Kit"

**Thesis:** For first-time buyers, reduce decision anxiety with a curated starter package — one Mira Bra + a sample-size Magic Body Oil + a sizing guide. Make the first purchase easy.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** "New to Mira? Start here." — product walkthrough of the starter kit
- **Static:** Starter kit product photo with contents labeled — "Everything you need to start"
- **Email:** Welcome flow for new subscribers — starter kit featured prominently

**Hooks:**
- "Not sure where to start? We built this for you."
- "Your first Mira experience, curated."
- "The starter kit that turned skeptics into repeat buyers."

**Metrics:** First-purchase conversion rate, AOV uplift vs. single-bra purchase, Retention rate (do starter kit buyers return?)

**Risk:** If the body oil sample is too small to experience properly, it becomes a throwaway and cheapens the bundle.

### 7.8 — "The Loyalty Unlock"

**Thesis:** Create a loyalty program where repeat Mira purchases unlock progressively better offers — second bra at 15% off, third at 20%, plus early access to new products.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** "What happens after your first Mira" — unlocking rewards, early access, VIP status
- **Static:** Loyalty tier visualization — "First bra → insider → VIP → founding member"
- **Email:** Post-purchase sequence introducing the loyalty program

**Hooks:**
- "Your first Mira was just the beginning."
- "Buy one. Love it. The second one's cheaper."
- "Welcome to the Mira club. Here's what you unlock next."

**Metrics:** Repeat purchase rate, LTV, Loyalty program enrollment rate

**Risk:** Loyalty programs require infrastructure and ongoing management. Don't promise what can't be maintained.

### 7.9 — "The Referral Loop"

**Thesis:** Women who love their bra tell their friends. Formalize this with a referral program — give $X, get $X. Turn satisfied customers into an acquisition channel.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** "My friend told me about this bra. Now I'm telling you." — chain-of-recommendation narrative
- **Static:** "Give $15. Get $15. Share the confidence."
- **Email:** Post-purchase referral invitation — "Know someone who needs this?"

**Hooks:**
- "She told her. She told her. She told her. That's how we grow."
- "The best marketing we have is the friend who sent you here."
- "Your friend already found it. Now it's your turn."

**Metrics:** Referral conversion rate, CAC reduction, Viral coefficient

**Risk:** Referral discounts must be sustainable. If the margin doesn't support $15/$15, adjust the offer.

### 7.10 — "The Subscription Body Oil"

**Thesis:** Magic Body Oil is a consumable — it runs out. A subscription model (auto-ship every 6-8 weeks) creates predictable recurring revenue and anchors the customer relationship beyond the initial bra purchase.

**Formats:**
- **Video:** "The thing I never have to remember to reorder" — subscription convenience
- **Static:** "Every 6 weeks. Fresh body oil. No reordering. No running out."
- **Email:** Post-body-oil-purchase offer — "Never run out again. Subscribe and save 15%."

**Hooks:**
- "Subscribe once. Soft skin forever."
- "The only subscription worth keeping."
- "15% off every bottle, delivered to your door, without thinking about it."

**Metrics:** Subscription enrollment rate, MRR (monthly recurring revenue), Churn rate, LTV

**Risk:** Subscription fatigue is real — consumers are overwhelmed with subscriptions. Must offer genuine convenience + savings to justify.
