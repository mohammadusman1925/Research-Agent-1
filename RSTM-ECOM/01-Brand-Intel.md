## 6. The Hiring Need — Creative Strategist (Meta Ads)

**Role:** Creative Strategist Meta Ads — Remote  
**Status:** Active (applied, in assessment pipeline)

### What They Want

1. **Own the creative strategy** for paid ads — tied to campaign objectives and growth KPIs
2. **Translate performance data** into creative hypotheses and clear briefs
3. **Write high-performing ad copy** for Meta Ads across video, UGC, static
4. **Adapt messaging** to different funnel stages and formats
5. **A/B testing plans** — creatives, headlines, CTAs, messaging angles
6. **Analyze performance data** — identify patterns, optimize creative direction
7. **Ensure cultural relevance** across European languages/markets
8. **Partner with design, growth, and content teams** for strategic consistency

### Requirements

- 3+ years in creative strategy, performance marketing, or direct-response advertising
- Proven track record of performance-oriented ad copy
- Familiarity with KPIs: CTR, ROAS, CAC, retention

### Nice to Have

- DTC / eCommerce / agency experience
- Localization strategy for European markets
- Background in branding, psychology, or storytelling

## 7. Strategic Assessment — What RSTM Needs vs. What Most Applicants Offer

### The Gap Most Creative Strategists Leave Open

| What RSTM Needs | What Most Applicants Offer |
|---|---|
| Data-driven creative decisions tied to KPIs | Mood boards and "brand voice" documents |
| Creative hypotheses from performance data | Copy written from gut feeling |
| Systematic A/B testing architecture | Random "let's test this" suggestions |
| Cross-format execution (video, UGC, static) | Strength in one format only |
| European market localization awareness | US-only thinking |
| Partnership with design + growth teams | Siloed creative briefs thrown over the wall |
| Institutional knowledge preservation | Every strategist starts from zero |

### What Makes This Role Different

RSTM operates their OWN brands at 8 figures. This is not an agency where you write briefs for clients you never meet. The Creative Strategist here is embedded in the P&L — every creative decision has a direct revenue consequence.

They need someone who:
- Reads CPM, hook rate, hold rate, CPA, CTR — and knows what each metric means for the next creative
- Understands the algorithm's creative preferences (not just "best practices")
- Can work across the full funnel — not just top-of-funnel awareness
- Documents everything so creative DNA compounds over time instead of resetting with each campaign

## 8. Competitive Landscape

RSTM competes for this hire against:
- In-house creative teams at funded DTC brands (better pay, narrower scope)
- Agencies offering "creative strategist" titles (broader scope, less ownership)
- Freelancers pitching project-based creative work (no system, no continuity)

**The candidate who wins this role** will be the one who demonstrates they can think in systems, not campaigns — and who proves they understand the science behind creative performance, not just the art.
