## R — Research: Market Context & Competitive Positioning

### Where RSTM Sits in the Market

RSTM is a rare hybrid: brand operator + service provider. They're not an agency selling time — they're operators with skin in the game. Every creative decision affects their own revenue.

This creates a fundamentally different creative environment:
- **Feedback loops are immediate** — you see the ROAS impact of your creative within days, not months
- **There's no client approval bottleneck** — the team decides, ships, and iterates
- **Creative DNA compounds** — the same team runs the same brands, so learning accumulates

### The European DTC Creative Landscape

European DTC is structurally different from US DTC:
- **CPMs vary dramatically** by country and language
- **Cultural nuance** matters more — a hook that works in Germany may fail in Austria or Sweden
- **Sustainability messaging** is table stakes, not a differentiator — RSTM's mission already reflects this
- **UGC quality expectations** are higher in European markets — less tolerance for obviously fake testimonials

### What the Best Creative Strategists in This Space Do

The top 1% of creative strategists working with European eCom operators:
1. **Read CPM as a creative signal** — high CPM isn't just "expensive audience," it's Meta telling you the creative isn't resonating
2. **Build creative testing trees** — not random A/B tests but systematic exploration of hooks, formats, angles, and offers
3. **Maintain creative DNA libraries** — every winning element (sound, visual, text, headline) is documented and reusable
4. **Think in clusters, not campaigns** — one concept spawns 5-10 variations across formats before declaring a winner or loser
5. **Connect creative to funnel stage** — TOF creative has different jobs than MOF or BOF creative

At €15M+ with 100% YoY growth, they've proven the media buying works. The constraint is how fast they can produce winning creative. A Creative Strategist who can:
- Reduce time from data signal → creative brief → production → live

...is worth more than another media buyer.

### Insight 2: The Role is Actually "Creative Systems Architect," Not Just "Strategist"

Reading the job description carefully, RSTM doesn't want someone who writes briefs. They want someone who:
- Builds the SYSTEM for generating briefs from data
- Creates repeatable processes the design team can execute without bottlenecks
- Documents patterns so the organization learns, not just the individual

This is the difference between a strategist who leaves and the company resets to zero, versus a strategist who builds an engine that outlasts them.

### Insight 3: The European Localization Angle is an Underpriced Skill

Most creative strategists applying for this role will be strong on Meta Ads copy. Few will understand:
- How to adapt creative frameworks across German, Austrian, Swedish, and other European markets
- Why the same product story needs different emotional entry points by culture
- How to build creative testing structures that account for market-specific CPM dynamics
