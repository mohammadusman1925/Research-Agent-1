# Competitor Marketplace Research: Hiring Creative Strategists & Media Buyers for DTC

*Research date: August 8, 2026*

---

## 1. Upwork

### Positioning
General freelance marketplace -- not DTC-specialized. Largest talent pool but zero curation for marketing quality. You post, you screen, you manage.

### Pricing
- **Creative Strategists (hourly):** $30-$75/hr mid-level, $75-$150+/hr senior
- **Creative Strategists (fixed/retainer):** $1,500/mo part-time to $2,500 per project (mid-level); $8,000-$10,000/mo for senior/Creative Director track
- **Media Buyers (hourly):** $50-$150/hr, with top-tier experts at $183/hr+
- **Platform fee:** 10% service fee on client side (freelancers pay 10% on first $10K earned per client)

### Quality of Talent
- Genuine DTC specialists exist: freelancers with 8+ years marketing, 2+ years direct response, experience with brands spending $10K-$300K/mo on Meta
- Profiles show UGC scripts, landing page copy, creative testing for Meta/TikTok
- **Major signal-to-noise problem.** Many generalists list "Facebook Ads" or "Creative Strategy" without real DTC performance experience. The creative strategist role for DTC is niche and new -- many Upwork freelancers listing it are actually social media managers or graphic designers who rebranded.

### Hiring Experience
- Post a job, receive proposals (often within 24 hours)
- You filter, interview, and vet entirely on your own
- No quality guarantee from the platform
- Speed is fast but quality control is your burden

### Reviews & Complaints
- **Dispute resolution is expensive:** Arbitration costs ~$675, sometimes more than the project itself
- **Customer service widely criticized:** Multiple support tickets, zero meaningful help
- **Account suspensions and fund withholding** are common complaints
- **Fraudulent activity:** Fake job postings and scam freelancers exist
- **Feedback manipulation:** Freelancers retaliate against honest reviews, inflating ratings
- Specialized platforms like Hiry explicitly advise: "If you need to hire on a tight budget you can consider Upwork but you should not use these platforms otherwise."

### Bottom Line
Best for budget-conscious hiring where you're willing to invest time in vetting. The $1,500/mo part-time creative strategist price point exists. But you carry all the risk.

## 2. MarketerHire

### Positioning
"The #1 talent platform for hiring marketers." Tagline: **"Hire expert marketers, on-demand."** Also uses: **"No job postings, No interviews, No headache."** Mission: "make expert marketing accessible to everyone." Serves startups through Fortune 500. Creative Strategist is explicitly listed among their roles.

### Pricing
Subscription-based model (moved away from hourly):

| Plan | Monthly Cost | Target |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Starter | $5,000+/mo | Testing new channels or first marketer hire |
| Growth | $10,000+/mo | Expanding across multiple channels |
| Scale | $15,000+/mo | Executive-level expertise to scale rapidly |

- No upfront deposit fees
- No monthly platform fees
- Claims no markup on freelancer rates
- Every hire includes a **two-week trial period**
- Custom plans available for full-time

### Vetting & Talent Quality
- **Under 1% acceptance rate** (some sources say top 5%)
- Multi-stage vetting: experience review, skill-specific assessment by specialists, structured video interview, **1-3 week test projects** in simulated environments
- Marketers must maintain perfect client satisfaction to stay in the network
- Matched talent described as "feeling like experienced in-house hires"

### How It Works
1. **30-min scoping call** with a "Growth Manager" (marketing professional, not recruiter)
2. AI-powered matching based on your specific problems -- matches on relevant experience, not keyword matching
3. Meet the matched marketer before committing (free)
4. Growth Manager stays involved for ongoing quality checks
5. **Matched within 48 hours to 1 week** typically

### Reviews
- **Trustpilot: 4.8/5** with 4,000+ reviews
- Praised for: speed of matching, talent quality, Growth Manager support, tangible results within 30-60 days
- G2: "excels in providing experienced marketing professionals swiftly, supporting diverse business needs with flexible terms"

### Complaints
1. **Price is steep for small companies** -- $5K/mo minimum is the #1 complaint
2. **Freelancer commission reduces take-home** -- talent may earn less than their posted rate
3. **Match misses** -- described as "real but rare," free rematch available
4. **Not for bargain seekers** -- multiple reviews emphasize this point
5. Small fraction of reviews cite customer service concerns

### Bottom Line
Built for companies spending $20K-$100K+/mo on marketing who need senior talent fast. At $5K/mo minimum ($60K/yr for lowest tier), it's priced for brands with significant ad budgets. The vetting and managed experience are genuinely differentiated from Upwork.

## 3. Mayple

### Positioning
AI-powered, data-driven matching platform connecting businesses with marketing professionals and boutique agencies. Emphasizes measurable campaign results over profile reviews. Uses performance data to vet and match.

### Pricing
- **Email marketing packages:** Start at $1,800/mo (includes audits, strategy, templates, optimization)
- **Marketing strategy:** Starts at $5,250
- **Consultations:** Start at $1,600/mo
- **Broader range:** $5K-$20K/mo, comparable to hiring a boutique agency
- **Minimum project size:** $500+

### Vetting & Talent Quality
- Claims **top 1% pass rate** with data-driven performance evaluation
- AI-assisted matching workflows
- Dedicated strategist support included with engagements
- Performance-focused vetting using actual campaign data

### How It Works
1. Create a marketing brief
2. AI-powered matching pairs you based on industry, business size, and goals
3. Meet your marketing match
4. Receive a customized marketing plan
5. Launch campaigns with ongoing monitoring
6. Transparent reporting system with real-time results

### Services
Social media management, paid advertising, SEO, influencer marketing, email marketing, and full marketing strategy.

### Reviews
- Listed on G2, Capterra, GetApp, SoftwareAdvice
- Praised for: strong AI matching, performance accountability, dedicated strategist support
- Positioned well for small and mid-size businesses

### Complaints
- **Expensive for smaller budgets** -- $5K-$20K/mo range prices out many SMBs
- **Less ideal for small projects** -- built for ongoing engagements
- **Upfront commitments may reduce flexibility** -- not great for testing
- Less transparency on exact pricing compared to MarketerHire

### Bottom Line
Mid-market positioning between Upwork and MarketerHire. More performance-data-driven than competitors. Good for businesses that want agency-level service with freelancer flexibility, but the $5K+ starting point is still steep for small budgets.

## 4. Growth Collective

### Positioning
Exclusive network of freelance marketers helping companies grow "without the agency markup." Specializes in growth marketing for startups and tech companies. Focus on full-funnel experimentation, conversion optimization, and paid acquisition. **Acquired by Toptal in June 2024.**

### Pricing
- **Not publicly disclosed** -- rates are freelancer-set
- Rates fall in the "mid to high professional range" reflecting experienced talent
- Most freelancers charge hourly or retainer-based
- Higher costs than general freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr)
- Pricing depends on freelancer experience, role type, and project size

### Vetting & Talent Quality
- Structured vetting: interviews, portfolio checks, past client results, reference reviews
- Most freelancers have "strong professional backgrounds from agencies, high-growth businesses, or well-known companies"
- Specialists include paid media, growth strategy, SEO, content
- Focus on marketers who can do both strategy and execution

### How It Works
1. Describe your goals and specialist needs
2. Receive personalized recommendations from vetted candidates
3. Select a freelancer
4. Platform handles contracts and payments
5. Collaborate via client portal

### Reviews
- **Trustpilot:** Positive reviews praising "amazing group of expert marketers"
- Clients note fast pairing with experienced talent and smooth operational handling
- Praised for: pre-screened quality, wide specialty range, fast matching, flexible scaling

### Complaints
- **Small company with limited, North America-based talent pool**
- **Not suitable for basic or low-cost tasks** -- priced for experienced talent
- **Less ongoing oversight compared to Mayple** -- you manage the relationship more directly
- **Narrow specialization** -- better suited for growth/tech companies than traditional DTC
- May not accommodate all role requirements or international projects
- Now owned by Toptal, which may change the experience

### Bottom Line
Good middle ground between Upwork chaos and MarketerHire premium pricing. Pre-vetted talent without the $5K/mo minimum. But the Toptal acquisition introduces uncertainty, the talent pool is smaller, and oversight is lighter than managed platforms.

## Pricing Benchmarks: What Do People Actually Charge?

### Freelance Creative Strategists

| Experience Level | Hourly Rate | Monthly Retainer |
|-----------------|-------------|------------------|
| Junior (0-2 yrs) | $50-70/hr | $3,000-$5,000/mo |
| Mid-level (2-5 yrs) | $70-100/hr | $5,000-$8,000/mo |
| Senior (5-8 yrs) | $100-135/hr | $8,000-$12,000/mo |
| Expert/Head (8+ yrs) | $135-175/hr | $12,000-$18,000/mo |

Full-time salary equivalents:
- Junior: $36,000-$75,000/yr
- Mid-level: $75,000-$130,000/yr
- Senior/Lead: $110,000-$165,000/yr
- Top tier (8+ yrs): $130,000-$250,000+/yr
- Offshore (USD-pegged): $28,800-$36,000/yr

### Freelance Media Buyers

| Experience Level | Hourly Rate | Monthly Retainer |
|-----------------|-------------|------------------|
| Junior (1-2 yrs) | $30-60/hr | $1,500-$3,000/mo |
| Mid-level (3-5 yrs) | $60-100/hr | $3,000-$6,000/mo |
| Senior (5+ yrs) | $100-150/hr | $5,000-$12,000/mo |
| Top-tier expert (10+ yrs) | $150-183+/hr | $10,000-$15,000/mo |

Full-time salary equivalents:
- Junior: $50,000-$70,000/yr
- Mid-level: $70,000-$100,000/yr
- Senior: $100,000-$140,000/yr

Platform specialization ranges (full-time):
- Meta (FB/IG): $70,000-$120,000
- Google Ads: $65,000-$110,000
- TikTok Ads: $60,000-$100,000

### Agency Pricing (Creative Strategy)

| Tier | Hourly Rate | Monthly Retainer |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| Junior agency staff | $100-140/hr | $6,500-$10,000/mo |
| Mid-level | $140-200/hr | $9,000-$14,000/mo |
| Senior | $200-280/hr | $14,000-$22,000/mo |
| Expert/Head | $280-400/hr | $20,000-$32,000/mo |

Agencies typically apply **50-100% markups** on specialist rates.

### Agency Pricing (Media Buying)

| Tier | Monthly Cost |
|------|-------------|
| Small boutique | $3,000-$7,000/mo |
| Mid-market | $7,000-$15,000/mo |
| Premium specialist | $12,000-$30,000+/mo |

**Freelancers are typically 30-50% cheaper than agencies for equivalent scope.**

### What DTC Brands Spending $10K-$100K/mo on Ads Should Expect to Pay

**For a senior freelance media buyer managing Meta + Google at $100K/mo budget:**
$8,000-$12,000/mo

**Fully loaded team costs:**
- In-house creative pod: $20,000-$30,000/mo
- Agency retainer: $5,000-$25,000/mo
- Freelance network: $2,500-$10,000/mo

**Key benchmark:** You should consider hiring a dedicated media buyer when paid ad spend exceeds $10K/mo.

### Common Pricing Models
1. **Flat monthly retainer** -- Most common for ongoing relationships
2. **Hourly billing** -- Common on Upwork, less common for senior talent
3. **Percentage of ad spend** -- Flagged as a potential red flag when lacking performance accountability; common at agencies (10-20% of spend typical)
4. **Performance/revenue share** -- Rising trend; ROAS or MER-tied bonuses on top of base retainer
5. **Project-based** -- Common for campaign concepts, brand repositioning, messaging frameworks

### Compensation Trends (2026)
- Performance-based compensation is rising -- more brands offer revenue share or ROAS-tied bonuses
- Multi-platform expertise commands highest rates
- Media buyers who also understand creative strategy (copy, hooks, UGC briefs, A/B testing) earn premiums
- AI tool proficiency (AdCreative.ai, Motion, Triple Whale) increases market value
- "Creative has quietly become the primary growth lever" for DTC -- creative strategy is financial strategy in 2026
