# DRIVE Creative Strategy Framework — Full Structure

**This is the exact system. Every DRIVE Strategy deliverable must follow this structure. No shortcuts, no summaries, no skipping sections.**

DRIVE = **D**iscover → **R**eframe → **I**gnite → **V**ariate → **E**volve

Reference: RSTM ECOM DRIVE Strategy (the gold standard)

---

## PHASE 1: D — DISCOVER

### 1.1 Mass Desire Analysis
- Table format: Product | Surface want | Mass desire (the real purchase)
- The mass desire is NEVER the product. It's the identity, guilt, hope, or fear driving the purchase.
- Include a Schwartz quote or relevant framework quote.

### 1.2 Schwartz Sophistication Map
- 5-stage table (Stage 1-5) with definitions
- Mark where the brand sits with a tag/badge
- Include "Creative Implication" lock box explaining what this means for ad creative

### 1.3 Purchase Trigger Map (Life Force 8)
- ALL 8 LF8 biological drives mapped:
  1. Survival / life extension
  2. Food / drink enjoyment
  3. Freedom from fear/pain
  4. Sexual companionship
  5. Comfortable living
  6. Superiority / winning
  7. Care of loved ones
  8. Social approval
- Tag each as Primary / Secondary / Not applicable
- Include "Dominant triggers" summary in a lock box

### 1.4 ICP Profiles
- Each ICP gets a FULL card with:
  - Name and archetype title (e.g., "The Fibro Fighter — 'Alison'")
  - Tags: Awareness level, Sophistication stage, Dominant LF8
  - Table with these exact rows:
    - **Trigger event** — The specific moment that makes them search
    - **Mental state** — What they're feeling right now
    - **Belief blocking sale** — The one belief stopping them from buying
    - **Emotional doorway** — The emotion that opens the door to purchase
    - **Hook direction** — An actual hook line for this ICP
- Minimum 5 ICPs per brand

---

## PHASE 2: R — REFRAME

### 2.1 Problem Mechanism
- In a lock box: "The Reframe: The problem isn't [what they think]. The problem is [what it actually is]."
- Explain the reframe in 3-4 sentences. This is the core strategic insight.

### 2.2 "Why Other Approaches Fail" Table
- Columns: Approach | What they claim | Why it fails | [Brand] advantage
- Include 4-5 competing approaches

### 2.3 Category Gap Analysis
- Table: Category norm | The gap | How [brand] fills it

### 2.4 Solution Mechanism (3-Layer)
- Table with 3 rows:
  - Layer 1: Surface mechanism (what the product does)
  - Layer 2: Unique mechanism (why this product does it differently)
  - Layer 3: Hidden mechanism (the identity/community/behavioral layer)
- Each layer gets: What it is | How to communicate

### 2.5 Four-Line Reframe Chains
- EXACTLY 4 lines per chain:
  - Line 1: Acknowledge current belief (Validate)
  - Line 2: Introduce the crack (Crack)
  - Line 3: Reframe the problem (Reframe)
  - Line 4: Bridge to the product (Bridge)
- Minimum 3 reframe chains per brand
- Each in a styled card/box

### 2.6 Skepticism Pre-emption
- Table: Objection | Pre-emption strategy
- Cover the top 5 objections

---

## PHASE 3: I — IGNITE

### 3.1 Emotional Driver Matrix
- Table: ICP | Primary emotion | Secondary emotion | Emotional peak moment | Creative format that ignites it
- Every ICP gets a row

### 3.2 Proof Architecture
- Hierarchy of proof types ranked by trust weight
- Map specific proof assets to each level

### 3.3 Conversion Amplifiers
- Urgency frames (price, stock, pain, risk reversal)
- CTA stack (the complete argument in order)

---

## PHASE 4: V — VARIATE

### 4.1 Hook Variations by Angle
- Grouped by angle (e.g., "Pain Relief," "Hotel Bed," "Price/Value")
- 4-6 hooks per angle
- Mark proven hooks from winning scripts

### 4.2 Format Variations
- Table: Format | Best for | Length | Key element
- Cover: UGC testimonial, review-style, couple reaction, unboxing, before/after, authority, comparison, ASMR

### 4.3 Testing Hierarchy
- Prioritized test plan: what to test first, variable, hypothesis
- Minimum 5 tests

### 4.4 Content Calendar
- 8-12 week sprint plan
- Table: Week | Deliverable | Angle | Format

---

## PHASE 5: E — EVOLVE

### 5.1 Fatigue Signals
- List of signals that creative is dying

### 5.2 Scale Triggers
- List of signals to increase spend

### 5.3 Sophistication Escalation Path
- Table: Stage | When | Creative shift
- Map the journey from current stage through Stage 5

## BIG IDEA FORMULATION & SCORING

### Scoring Criteria (each /5, total /25):
1. **Specificity** — Can only this brand make this claim?
2. **Emotional charge** — Does it stop the scroll?
3. **Proof-ability** — Can it be demonstrated visually?
4. **Scalability** — How many variations can it generate?
5. **Reframe power** — Does it change what the prospect thinks the problem is?

### Score thresholds:
- Above 18: Worth testing
- Above 21: Worth scaling
- Below 15: Rework

Minimum 3 Big Ideas per brand, each fully scored.

## APPENDIX A: HEADLINE LIBRARY (70+)

- Organized by angle in collapsible sections
- Each angle gets 5-8 headlines
- Headlines are production-ready: usable as Meta ad primary text, video hooks, or email subject lines
- Target: 70+ total headlines

---

## APPENDIX B: COMPLIANCE NOTES

- Table: Risk area | Specific risk | Mitigation
- Cover: Health claims (FDA/FTC), Meta ad policy, testimonial rules, before/after restrictions, pricing claims
- Platform-specific: Meta, TikTok, Google

---

## APPENDIX C: METRICS FRAMEWORK

- Table: Metric | What it measures | Benchmark | Creative action if below benchmark
- Cover: Hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA, ROAS, creative fatigue indicators, win rate
- Include a dashboard view with stat cards
